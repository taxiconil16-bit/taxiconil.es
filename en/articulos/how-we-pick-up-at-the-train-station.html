At Taxi Conil, we offer a train station pickup service designed to make your arrival hassle-free. We wait for you at the exit, help with luggage, and take you directly to your destination. We explain how it works.

How does our train station pickup system work?

From the moment you make your reservation until we pick you up at the station, our system is designed to guarantee a hassle-free experience:

1. Email confirmation

After making your reservation, you will receive an automatic confirmation email with all the details. If you don't receive it within 12 hours, check your Spam folder.

2. WhatsApp reminder

The day before your trip, between 19:00 and 22:00, we will send you a WhatsApp reminder with your driver's information.

3. Train verification

We verify the actual arrival time. If there are minor delays, we adjust the pickup time at no extra cost.

4. Pickup with identification

On the day of your reservation, the driver will be waiting for you at the station exit with a device or sign with your name visible.

Courtesy time: We wait as long as necessary for you to have time to get off the train and collect your luggage. Our drivers will help you load and unload your luggage in the vehicle.

Resolving Doubts

Frequently Asked Questions

How much courtesy time do you have?
We wait as long as necessary for you to have time to get off the train and collect your luggage.
Do you charge for train delays?
For minor delays (up to 30 minutes), we don't charge. For longer delays, contact us to coordinate.
Where do you pick me up at the station?
We wait for you at the main exit, near the exit turnstiles or in the taxi area, depending on the station.
Is it mandatory to give the train number?
It is not mandatory, but highly recommended. Without the train number, we cannot adjust the pickup time if there are delays.
What if my train is cancelled?
If your train is cancelled, contact us as soon as possible to reschedule or cancel your reservation at no cost.
Can I change the pickup time?
Yes, you can change the pickup time by contacting us by phone or WhatsApp at least 2 hours in advance.
Does the driver help with luggage?
Yes, our drivers will help you load and unload your luggage in the vehicle at no extra cost.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash, Bizum, Visa and Mastercard cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
